Is Immigrating to Canada Still Worth It in 2026? My Honest 8-Year Review

✍️ Anuj Vaghela 📅 May 2026 ⏱ 4 min read

This question is likely keeping you up at night. After 8.5 years of building a life here — moving from India to Ontario in 2017 on temporary status — I'm ready to give you the truth.

This isn't a government brochure or a consultant's sales pitch. These are my personal observations, shaped by nearly a decade of navigating the Canadian immigration system as a student, worker, and permanent resident.

Packing your entire life into two suitcases can be a massive gamble. To help you decide if it's the right one for you in 2026, I've broken down the "Canadian Dream" into 16 real-world categories.

A note on the comparison

My goal is to compare life in India and Canada to provide clarity — not to demean either country. I've spent a significant chunk of my life in both, and I know that "better" is subjective.

💡 I will avoid overwhelming you with government statistics. Instead, I'll focus on perspective — the kind you only get after 3,000+ days on the ground.

Final thoughts

The Canada of 2026 is not the Canada of 2018. The rules have changed, the costs have risen, but the opportunities for those with a specialized skillset are still there.
