Why I started this

Behind the project

Every immigration blog I found was either a consultant's sales pitch, a thread full of outdated advice, or a confusing government PDF.

So I started writing. Not to be an authority, but to share the kind of perspective you'd get from a friend who's already been through it. The friend who tells you to bring more warm clothes, warns you about the survival job trap, and is honest about the moments that made them question everything.

Unfiltered Canada is that friend. Whether you're still outside Canada weighing your options, freshly landed and overwhelmed, or years deep and wondering if you made the right call, this site is written for you.

💡 Disclaimer: Everything on this site is my personal experience and opinion. It is not immigration, legal, or financial advice. Always verify details with official sources, like IRCC, before making any decisions.

What you'll find here

The core roadmap

Deep dives into the 16 factors that actually determine whether Canada is worth it for you. My observations on education ROI, job market realities, cost of living in Ontario, the permanent residency grind, and the things most people don't talk about publicly, like racism and mental health.

I write one post at a time, as thoroughly as I can, prioritising the topics my readers ask about most. No filler, no affiliate links, no consultant agenda.
