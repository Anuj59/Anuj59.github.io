Why I started this

Every immigration blog I found was either a consultant's sales pitch, a Reddit thread full of outdated advice, or a government PDF designed to confuse you.

So I started writing. Not to be an authority — but to share the kind of perspective you'd get from a friend who's already been through it. The friend who tells you to bring more warm clothes, warns you about the survival job trap, and is honest about the moments that made them question everything.

Unfiltered Canada is that friend.

💡 Everything on this site is my personal experience and opinion. It is not immigration, legal, or financial advice. Always verify details with official sources before making decisions.

What you'll find here

Deep dives into the 16 factors that actually determine whether Canada is worth it for you — education ROI, job market realities, cost of living, the PR grind, and the things people don't talk about publicly, like racism and mental health.

I write one post at a time, as thoroughly as I can, prioritising the topics my readers ask about most.
